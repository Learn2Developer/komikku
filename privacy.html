Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy describes the KomikkuToo fork maintained at Learn2Developer/komikku and this project website. Features and data flows can differ in other forks or builds.

Data on your device

The app stores library entries, categories, chapter and reading progress, history, reader settings, source configuration, and cached information locally. Optional downloads and backups are stored in the locations you choose. Removing app data or uninstalling may remove local records; exported files can remain in shared storage.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you enable Drive sync, KomikkuToo requests the Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This permits the app to create, read, update, and delete its own hidden application data. It does not grant access to browse or edit your other Drive documents.

The app uploads a compressed library backup directly to your Google Drive app-data folder and downloads it to merge changes across devices. Depending on your settings, this can include library titles and identifiers, categories, chapters, read progress, history, tracking records, saved searches, feeds, and app or source preferences. Enabling private-settings backup may also include sensitive source or app settings such as saved credentials; review that option before enabling it.

Google Drive sign-in is handled by Google. The app receives authorization tokens, not your Google password. Browser-flow tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore on the device and excluded from the app's library backups and sync. Devices with Google Play Services can use Google's native authorization flow. Google processes authorization and cloud data under its own policies.

The project does not operate a separate server that receives your Drive library backup. Sync uses HTTPS transport, but the compressed backup is not additionally encrypted by KomikkuToo before uploading; this is not an end-to-end encrypted backup format. Drive access is used to provide sync and related data-management actions, not advertising. KomikkuToo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sources, ratings and release calendars

Using installed extensions sends requests directly to the source services they access. Online rating and release matching sends title searches and series or issue identifiers to the selected catalogue or release provider, such as MangaUpdates or League of Comic Geeks. Those services can receive normal connection data, including your IP address. Optional tracking services receive the reading information you choose to synchronize. Their own privacy policies apply.

Retention, disconnecting and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them or clear the app's data. Drive sync data remains in your Google account until deleted. The app's Data and storage settings include actions to delete its Drive sync data and disconnect Google Drive. Disconnecting authorization does not itself delete an existing cloud backup. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google Account permissions. When a token expires or is revoked, you may need to sign in again. Exported backups and downloaded files may require separate deletion.

This website and support

This static website has no application login, advertising scripts, or custom analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may log connection information, including visitor IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy policy. Public GitHub issues and comments are visible to others: do not include passwords, tokens, private backups, or personal reading data you do not want public.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the maintainer’s GitHub profile, without posting confidential information. This policy may be updated as the app changes; its effective date will be updated here.

KomikkuToo is an independent, community-derived Android app based on Komikku, Mihon and TachiyomiSY. It is not affiliated with Google or the comic and manga publishers.
