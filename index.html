Sync with your Drive

Optional Google Drive sync merges library data and reading progress between devices signed in to the same Google account.

Google Drive access, explained

KomikkuToo requests only drive.appdata access for its own hidden sync data. It does not request access to browse or edit your other Drive documents. Sign-in happens through Google, using browser authorization on devices without Google Play Services.

Sync data travels directly between your device and Google Drive. Its contents depend on the backup and sync settings you enable. Read the privacy policy for details about storage, optional private settings, and deleting data.

Bring your own content

The app does not provide a catalogue of hosted comics. Installed extensions and third-party services have their own availability, accounts, and policies. Use only content you have permission to access.

KomikkuToo is an independent, community-derived Android app based on Komikku, Mihon and TachiyomiSY. It is not affiliated with Google or the comic and manga publishers.
